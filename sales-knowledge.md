# PinPro sales knowledge

## Service boundaries

- Google Maps Local SEO improves a Google Business Profile's opportunity to appear in Google Maps and local search.
- Website SEO improves a website's opportunity to appear in ordinary website search results. PinPro does not offer this service.
- Google Ads is paid advertising. It is not SEO and is not included in Local SEO packages.
- Facebook or Instagram Check-in is separate from Google Maps and Local SEO. It costs 900 baht per platform and normally takes 7–15 days after review.

Never imply that pin creation, Facebook Check-in, Google Ads and Local SEO are included together unless the owner has prepared a combined quotation.

## Current Local SEO packages

- 14 days: 2,800 baht.
- 30 days: 4,500 baht.
- 1,900 baht is an obsolete historical price and must never be quoted to a new customer.

Do not guarantee a ranking, calls, directions, visits or sales. Results depend on distance, relevance, prominence, competition, reviews and Google's systems.

## Sales decision rules

1. Determine whether the customer wants Google Maps visibility, Website SEO, paid Google Ads or Facebook/Instagram Check-in.
2. For Google Maps visibility, request the Google Maps link, business name, target keywords and service area.
3. Before inspection, explain the service and collect details. Do not pretend an inspection has already happened.
4. After inspection, recommend one best-fit package. State price, duration, included work, excluded work, measurable reporting and the next step.
5. When a customer accepts, stop repeating discovery questions. Confirm the package and move to quotation/payment and starting requirements.
6. If a quotation or marketplace order is cancelled, acknowledge it, prepare a replacement quotation and follow up promptly.

## Example after inspection

From the initial inspection, the target keywords are not currently showing the business pin prominently in the main search results. The recommended option is the 30-day Google Maps Local SEO package at 4,500 baht.

The package covers improvements to business information, descriptions, services or products, posts, images, review guidance and area-based ranking tracking, with before/after reporting. It does not include Google Ads, Website SEO or Facebook Check-in.

Ranking cannot be guaranteed because results depend on distance, relevance, prominence, competitors and Google's systems. If the customer agrees, proceed with a quotation for this package and the starting requirements.
